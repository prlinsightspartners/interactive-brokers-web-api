Methodology and limitations

  • Uses current IBKR position market values and up to one year of daily close-to-close returns, compared with SPY. Portfolio weights are held constant over the historical window; this is a current-holdings proxy, not realized historical portfolio performance. Cash and any unmodeled exposure are implicitly assigned zero return.
  • Systematic and idiosyncratic volatility are estimated by a single-factor linear regression. Idiosyncratic risk is the regression residual; it can still include other shared risk factors.
  • VaR is a parametric one-day 95% estimate based on the fitted portfolio-return sample. It assumes a roughly normal return distribution and can understate gap, tail, liquidity, and concentration risk.
  • Market-value weighting is not delta-adjusted. Option values reflect option premium rather than underlying-equivalent exposure; futures, FX, bonds, and non-US assets may not be well explained by SPY beta. FX movements, option Greeks, volatility/skew, rates, and nonlinear payoffs are not modeled.
  • Displayed market values and the account denominator are taken from IBKR. Estimates require sufficient overlapping price history; the modeled coverage figure reports the share of gross position market value included.
